# Third-party data and software

This repository contains evaluation material derived from
[`mit-oasys/oolong-pairs`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mit-oasys/oolong-pairs),
which is published under the MIT license. OOLONG-Pairs and the Recursive
Language Models method are by Alex Zhang, Tim Kraska and Omar Khattab (MIT
OASYS Lab).

The controlled baseline imports a separate, unmodified checkout of
[`alexzhang13/rlm`](https://github.com/alexzhang13/rlm) at the commit recorded in
the evaluation manifest. Upstream RLM is MIT-licensed and is not vendored into
this repository.

Model checkpoints are not redistributed here. Their source pages and licenses
are linked from the main README.
